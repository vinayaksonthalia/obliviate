Settings — Model

Obliviate is model-agnostic — bring your own local or hosted model. Extraction and grounded answering work with any provider.

Obliviate never couples forgetting to a specific vendor. The verifiable-erasure guarantee is enforced in CockroachDB, independent of which LLM performs extraction and answering.
